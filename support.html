常见问题

卸载后数据还在吗？不在。分刻只把记录存在当前设备，卸载或使用「清除所有数据」都会删除本机记录，且无法由开发者恢复。请定期在「更多 → 数据管理」导出备份。

iPhone 和 iPad 怎么同步？分刻没有云同步。请在旧设备导出 JSON 备份，再在新设备导入；导入会覆盖目标设备上的同份数据。

iPad 上布局为什么和 iPhone 不同？iPad 横屏为左右双栏，竖屏与 iPhone 一致为单列，属于设计口径，不是故障。

分刻 PLUS 有哪些档位，包含什么？在「更多 → 分刻 PLUS」中选择月度订阅（¥3 / 月）、年度订阅（¥28 / 年），或一次性买断（¥58，永久有效，含未来全部新增内容）。不提供免费试用。PLUS 仅解锁四款专属主题（大地探索、头号玩家、粉色记忆、墨银 · 液态金属）与自定义重点色。

哪些内容免费，哪些需要 PLUS？基础记录与创建功能永久免费。初始蓝主题，星星 · 初次见面、魔法师 · 百分百、小王子 · 一周年三款福利主题及对应应用图标，还有小刻瞬间装扮，都可通过完成对应任务永久免费解锁，无需付费。只有上述四款专属主题和自定义重点色需要 PLUS。

什么是创始人限定勋章和 2027 年度限定勋章？分刻的勋章分两类。创始人限定勋章包含星星人、魔法师、小王子三款装扮勋章以及生日勋章；2027 年度限定勋章包含 2027 地球日、2027 情人节、2027 新年三枚。命名带年份是为了避免与后续年度混淆。这些勋章本身免费解锁，其中年度勋章配套的应用图标需要 PLUS 才能设置。

创始人限定勋章的获取条件是什么？仅限 2027 年 1 月 31 日当天及之前从 App Store 下载安装分刻的用户激活。首次下载时间以 App Store 官方记录（AppTransaction）为准，删除重装也不会改变。逾期下载的用户无法激活这三款装扮勋章的解锁任务；生日勋章还需此外把任意一个生日设为纪念日或倒计时。激活资格一旦具备，后续进度达标即可解锁，已解锁的永久保留。

2027 年度限定勋章怎么解锁？按你首次下载 App 的时间判定：在对应节日前后一周内完成下载即可激活。具体截止为——2027 新年：2027 年 1 月 8 日及之前；2027 情人节：2027 年 2 月 21 日及之前；2027 地球日：2027 年 4 月 29 日及之前。晚于对应日期下载的用户无法激活该勋章。已解锁的勋章永久保留，不受后续时间影响。

首次下载时间是怎么核对的，会读取我的隐私数据吗？分刻使用 Apple 官方 StoreKit 提供的 AppTransaction 凭据读取首次下载日期，该凭据由 App Store 签名，仅用于判断限定勋章的激活资格，不上传、不传给任何服务器。若无法取得凭据（例如 TestFlight 或开发版安装），按宽松口径处理，不影响使用。如需重置设备时间，不影响已解锁的勋章。

如何取消订阅或申请退款？购买、取消订阅与退款均由 Apple 按官方政策统一管理，分刻不介入。取消订阅：iOS「设置 → 你的 Apple 账户 → 订阅」。申请退款：通过 Apple 官方渠道 reportaproblem.apple.com 提交，是否可退以 Apple 审核结果为准。

订阅到期后我的数据会怎样？你创建的所有记录、days / notes 的个性化配置，以及通过任务解锁的主题、图标和小刻瞬间装扮都会永久保留，不受订阅状态影响，也不会被回收。仅四款 PLUS 专属主题与自定义重点色在订阅到期后恢复默认，需重新订阅或用买断解锁；一次性买断永久有效，无到期问题。

iPhone 和 iPad 都要付费吗？同一 Apple 账户下 iPhone 与 iPad 共享购买状态，在任一设备购买后，另一设备进入「更多 → 我的分刻」点「恢复购买」即可激活，无需重复付费。分刻目前仅支持 iOS / iPadOS，暂不支持 Mac 端。

Countmin is a time-journal app that runs entirely on your device. There is no account, no server, and no data collection. When reporting an issue, please include your device model, iOS version, app version, and steps to reproduce.

Frequently asked questions

Is my data kept after uninstalling? No. Records live only on the current device. Uninstalling or using "Clear All Data" deletes them permanently, and the developer cannot restore them. Export a backup regularly under "More → Data Management".

How do I sync between iPhone and iPad? Countmin has no cloud sync. Export a JSON backup on the old device and import it on the new one; importing overwrites the matching data on the target device.

Why does iPad look different from iPhone? iPad uses a two-column layout in landscape and the same single-column layout as iPhone in portrait. This is intentional, not a bug.

What does Countmin PLUS include? Under "More → Countmin PLUS", choose a monthly subscription (¥3/month), an annual subscription (¥28/year), or a one-time lifetime purchase (¥58, permanent and including all future additions). No free trial is offered. PLUS unlocks only four exclusive themes (Earth Explorer, Player One, Pink Memory, and Ink Silver · Liquid Metal) plus a custom accent color.

What is free and what requires PLUS? Basic journaling and creation are free forever. Original Blue, the three welfare themes — Star · First Meeting, Magician · Hundred Percent, and Little Prince · First anniversary — and their matching app icons, as well as Xiaoke Moment outfits, can all be unlocked permanently for free by completing the corresponding tasks. Only the four exclusive themes and the custom accent color above require PLUS.

What are Founder's Edition and 2027 Annual Edition badges? Countmin badges come in two groups. Founder's Edition badges include the three outfits — Star, Magician, and Little Prince — plus the Birthday badge. The 2027 Annual Edition badges are 2027 Earth Day, 2027 Valentine's Day, and 2027 New Year. The year prefix avoids confusion with future editions. These badges unlock for free; only the matching app icons for the annual badges require PLUS to apply.

How do I qualify for a Founder's Edition badge? Available only to users who downloaded Countmin from the App Store on or before January 31, 2027. Your first-download date is taken from Apple's official AppTransaction record, which does not change if you delete and reinstall the app. Users who download after that date cannot activate the unlock tasks for the three outfits; the Birthday badge additionally requires setting any birthday as an anniversary or countdown. Once eligible, progress toward the tasks unlocks the badges, and unlocked badges are kept forever.

How do I unlock a 2027 Annual Edition badge? Eligibility is based on when you first downloaded the app: downloading within one week before or after the corresponding event activates that badge. The deadlines are — 2027 New Year: on or before January 8, 2027; 2027 Valentine's Day: on or before February 21, 2027; 2027 Earth Day: on or before April 29, 2027. Downloads after the respective date cannot activate that badge. Unlocked badges are kept permanently and are unaffected by later dates.

How is the first-download date verified, and does it access my private data? Countmin reads the first-download date from Apple's official StoreKit AppTransaction credential, which is signed by the App Store. It is used only to determine badge eligibility — it is never uploaded or sent to any server. If the credential is unavailable (for example, TestFlight or development builds), the app takes the permissive path so nothing is blocked. Changing your device's date does not affect badges you have already unlocked.

How do I cancel a subscription or request a refund? All purchases, cancellations, and refunds are managed by Apple under its official policies; Countmin is not involved. To cancel: iOS "Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions". To request a refund: submit via Apple's official channel at reportaproblem.apple.com; eligibility is determined by Apple.

What happens to my data when a subscription expires? All records you created, your days / notes personalization settings, and any themes, icons, and Xiaoke outfits unlocked through tasks are kept permanently regardless of subscription status and are never reclaimed. Only the four PLUS-exclusive themes and the custom accent color revert to default, requiring re-subscription or the lifetime purchase to unlock again. The lifetime purchase never expires.

Do I need to pay on both iPhone and iPad? Purchases are shared between iPhone and iPad on the same Apple Account. After buying on either device, tap "Restore Purchases" under "More → My Countmin" on the other — no need to pay twice. Countmin currently supports iOS / iPadOS only; a Mac version is not yet available.
