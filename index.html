Countmin · Privacy Policy

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 18 日　·　最近更新：2026 年 11 月 21 日
Effective: November 18, 2026　·　Last updated: November 21, 2026
一句话总结：分刻是一款以本机存储为主的时间手帐应用。我们不收集、不分析、不共享你的任何个人信息，也没有账号系统或自有服务器。你可选择用 Apple 官方 iCloud 在自己的 iPhone 与 iPad 之间同步记录——那是 Apple 的系统服务，不是分刻在联网上传。

一、我们收集哪些信息

不收集任何信息。分刻没有账号系统，不需要你注册或登录，也不要求提供姓名、电话号码、电子邮箱、住址或其他任何个人身份信息。

应用不包含任何广告、数据分析、用户行为追踪或第三方软件开发工具包（SDK），不会在后台向开发者的服务器发送你的数据。开发者无法访问你的任何记录。

二、数据存放在哪里

你在使用中产生的全部内容——包括时间记录、倒计时、纪念日、心情笔记、主题偏好和界面设置——默认只保存在你的设备本地存储空间中。卸载应用会一并删除本机数据，且无法恢复，请按需自行备份。

关于 iCloud 同步（Apple 官方功能）：分刻使用 Apple 提供的 iCloud 键值存储（NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore）在你的 iPhone 与 iPad 之间同步同一份记录。这是 Apple 操作系统内置的服务：

  • 同步经由你自己的 iCloud 账户进行，数据只在你的设备与你的 iCloud 账户之间流转；
  • 分刻的开发者无法读取、访问或导出这些数据，也没有任何自有服务器参与；
  • 你未登录 iCloud、关闭 iCloud 同步或设备离线时，应用完全以单机方式运行，所有记录先存在本机，联网后由系统自动同步；
  • 若多台设备都修改过记录，以最后修改的一方为准。你也可以在「更多 → 数据管理」用本地 JSON 备份手动迁移。

三、关于照片与相册

当你选择用照片作为卡片背景时，应用会通过系统提供的照片选择器读取你主动选中的图片。该过程在系统提供的独立环境中完成：

  • 应用不会申请完整相册访问权限，也无法浏览你未选中的照片。
  • 选中的图片压缩后保存在本机；若你开启 iCloud 同步，会随其他记录一起经你的 iCloud 账户同步到你的其他设备，不会被上传到开发者的服务器或第三方服务。

四、网络与第三方服务

分刻的核心功能不需要网络连接，App 自身不发起任何联网请求、不申请网络权限、不含第三方统计、广告或社交登录服务，因此不存在向开发者或第三方共享数据的情形。

唯一涉及网络的是上述 Apple 官方 iCloud 同步：它由操作系统在你登录 iCloud 后代为执行，用于把你自己的数据同步到你自己的设备。这是 Apple 的功能，不是分刻应用自身的联网行为；你完全可以不使用它，仅在单机模式下使用全部核心功能。

五、儿童隐私

分刻不面向 13 岁以下的儿童，也不会有意收集任何儿童的个人信息。如果你发现儿童向我们提供了个人信息，请通过下方方式联系我们，我们会尽快删除。

六、数据的删除与控制

数据始终由你掌控，你可以随时自行管理：

  • 在应用中删除单条记录、笔记或卡片；删除项先进入回收站，保留期后可清空。
  • 在「更多 → 数据管理」导出 JSON 备份、导入备份，或查看 / 清空回收站。
  • 在「设置 → 关于」点击「清除所有数据」，会重置本机记录并同步清空你的 iCloud 副本。
  • 直接卸载应用会删除本机数据；如需同时清除 iCloud 副本，可在系统「设置 → Apple ID → iCloud」中管理分刻的云数据。

我们没有能力访问、查看、导出或恢复你设备或 iCloud 账户中的这些数据。

七、隐私政策的更新

如果未来应用的功能发生变化并涉及数据处理方式的调整，我们会更新本页面，并在应用内提供最新版本的隐私政策链接。更新后的政策自页面标注的生效日期起适用。

如果你对本隐私政策有任何疑问、意见或需要协助处理数据相关问题，可以通过开发者 GitHub 主页的公开 Issue 与我们联系：

In short: Countmin is a time-journal app that stores your data primarily on your device. We do not collect, analyze, or share any of your personal information, and there is no account system or server of our own. You may optionally use Apple's official iCloud to sync your records between your own iPhone and iPad — that is an Apple system service, not Countmin uploading data over the network.

1. What information we collect

None. Countmin has no account system: no registration or sign-in is required, and we never ask for your name, phone number, email address, home address, or any other personal identifier.

The app contains no advertising, analytics, user-behavior tracking, or third-party software development kits (SDKs), and never sends your data to a developer server in the background. The developer cannot access any of your records.

2. Where your data is stored

Everything you create while using the app — time records, countdowns, anniversaries, mood notes, theme preferences, and interface settings — is stored by default only in your device's local storage. Uninstalling the app removes the local data permanently and it cannot be recovered, so please back up as needed.

About iCloud sync (an official Apple feature): Countmin uses Apple's iCloud key-value storage (NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore) to sync the same set of records between your iPhone and iPad. This is a service built into Apple's operating system:

  • Syncing happens through your own iCloud account; data flows only between your devices and your iCloud account.
  • The developer of Countmin cannot read, access, or export this data, and no server of our own is involved.
  • If you are not signed in to iCloud, have iCloud sync disabled, or are offline, the app runs entirely offline: records are kept locally and synced by the system once you are back online.
  • If records were changed on multiple devices, the most recently modified version wins. You can also migrate manually via local JSON backup under "More → Data Management".

3. Photos and the photo library

When you choose a photo as a card background, the app reads only the image you explicitly select through the system photo picker. This takes place in the system's own isolated environment:

  • The app does not request full photo-library access and cannot browse photos you did not select.
  • The selected image is compressed and stored locally; if you have iCloud sync enabled, it syncs with your other records through your iCloud account to your other devices, and is never uploaded to a developer server or third-party service.

4. Network and third-party services

Countmin's core features require no network connection. The app itself makes no network requests, requests no network permission, and contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or social-login services, so there is no sharing of data with the developer or any third party.

The only network-related aspect is the official Apple iCloud sync described above: it is performed by the operating system after you sign in to iCloud, solely to sync your own data to your own devices. This is an Apple feature, not networking performed by the Countmin app itself; you can opt out entirely and use all core features offline.

5. Children's privacy

Countmin is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the method below and we will delete it promptly.

6. Deleting and controlling your data

Your data is always under your control, and you can manage it at any time:

  • Delete individual records, notes, or cards within the app; deleted items go to a trash and can be emptied after a retention period.
  • Use "More → Data Management" to export a JSON backup, import a backup, or view / empty the trash.
  • Tap "Clear All Data" under "Settings → About" to reset local records and clear your iCloud copy as well.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes local data; to remove the iCloud copy as well, manage Countmin's cloud data in the system "Settings → Apple ID → iCloud".

We have no ability to access, view, export, or recover this data on your device or in your iCloud account.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app's features change in a way that affects how data is handled, we will update this page and provide the latest privacy-policy link within the app. The updated policy applies from the effective date shown on this page.
