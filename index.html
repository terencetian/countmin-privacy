Countmin · Privacy Policy

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 18 日　·　最近更新：2026 年 11 月 22 日
Effective: November 18, 2026　·　Last updated: November 22, 2026
一句话总结：分刻是一款完全在本机运行的时间手帐应用。我们不收集、不分析、不共享你的任何个人信息，也没有账号系统、自有服务器或云同步。你的记录只保存在当前设备；如需跨设备迁移，请自行使用本地备份导出与导入。

一、我们收集哪些信息

不收集任何信息。分刻没有账号系统，不需要你注册或登录，也不要求提供姓名、电话号码、电子邮箱、住址或其他任何个人身份信息。

应用不包含任何广告、数据分析、用户行为追踪或第三方软件开发工具包（SDK），不会在后台向开发者的服务器发送你的数据。开发者无法访问你的任何记录。

二、数据存放在哪里

你在使用中产生的全部内容——包括时间记录、倒计时、纪念日、心情笔记、主题偏好和界面设置——默认只保存在你的设备本地存储空间中。卸载应用会一并删除本机数据，且无法恢复，请按需自行备份。

纯本机存储，不提供云同步。分刻不会把你的记录上传到任何服务器或云服务，也不使用 iCloud 或其他键值同步服务。

  • 所有记录仅保存在当前设备的本地存储中，开发者无法读取、访问或导出；
  • 应用没有账号系统、没有自有服务器，也不申请网络权限；
  • 如需在 iPhone 与 iPad 之间迁移，请在旧设备「更多 → 数据管理」导出 JSON 备份，再在新设备导入；
  • 导入会覆盖目标设备上的同份数据，请提前自行留好备份。

三、关于照片与相册

当你选择用照片作为卡片背景时，应用会通过系统提供的照片选择器读取你主动选中的图片。该过程在系统提供的独立环境中完成：

  • 应用不会申请完整相册访问权限，也无法浏览你未选中的照片。
  • 选中的图片压缩后只保存在本机，不会上传到开发者的服务器或第三方服务，也不会自动同步到其他设备。

四、网络与第三方服务

分刻的核心功能不需要网络连接，App 自身不发起任何联网请求、不申请网络权限、不含第三方统计、广告或社交登录服务，因此不存在向开发者或第三方共享数据的情形。

分刻没有云同步，也不存在由系统代行的数据上传。全部核心功能均可离线使用；跨设备迁移依赖你自己导出与导入的本地备份文件。

五、儿童隐私

分刻不面向 13 岁以下的儿童，也不会有意收集任何儿童的个人信息。如果你发现儿童向我们提供了个人信息，请通过下方方式联系我们，我们会尽快删除。

六、数据的删除与控制

数据始终由你掌控，你可以随时自行管理：

  • 在应用中删除单条记录、笔记或卡片；删除项先进入回收站，保留期后可清空。
  • 在「更多 → 数据管理」导出 JSON 备份、导入备份，或查看 / 清空回收站。
  • 在「设置 → 关于」点击「清除所有数据」，会重置本机记录；该操作不可撤销。
  • 直接卸载应用会删除本机数据。由于没有云同步，卸载后开发者也无法为你恢复。

我们没有能力访问、查看、导出或恢复你设备中的这些数据。

七、隐私政策的更新

如果未来应用的功能发生变化并涉及数据处理方式的调整，我们会更新本页面，并在应用内提供最新版本的隐私政策链接。更新后的政策自页面标注的生效日期起适用。

八、联系我们

如果你对本隐私政策有任何疑问、意见或需要协助处理数据相关问题，可以通过开发者 GitHub 主页的公开 Issue 与我们联系：

In short: Countmin runs entirely on your device. We do not collect, analyze, or share any of your personal information, and there is no account system, server of our own, or cloud sync. Your records stay on the current device; to move them to another device, export a local backup and import it yourself.

1. What information we collect

None. Countmin has no account system: no registration or sign-in is required, and we never ask for your name, phone number, email address, home address, or any other personal identifier.

The app contains no advertising, analytics, user-behavior tracking, or third-party software development kits (SDKs), and never sends your data to a developer server in the background. The developer cannot access any of your records.

2. Where your data is stored

Everything you create while using the app — time records, countdowns, anniversaries, mood notes, theme preferences, and interface settings — is stored by default only in your device's local storage. Uninstalling the app removes the local data permanently and it cannot be recovered, so please back up as needed.

Local-only storage, no cloud sync. Countmin never uploads your records to any server or cloud service, and does not use iCloud or any key-value sync service.

  • All records are stored only in this device's local storage; the developer cannot read, access, or export them.
  • There is no account system and no server of our own, and the app requests no network permission.
  • To migrate between iPhone and iPad, export a JSON backup under "More → Data Management" on the old device and import it on the new one.
  • Importing overwrites the matching data on the target device, so keep your own backup first.

3. Photos and the photo library

When you choose a photo as a card background, the app reads only the image you explicitly select through the system photo picker. This takes place in the system's own isolated environment:

  • The app does not request full photo-library access and cannot browse photos you did not select.
  • The selected image is compressed and stored locally only. It is never uploaded to a developer server or third-party service, and is never synced to another device automatically.

4. Network and third-party services

Countmin's core features require no network connection. The app itself makes no network requests, requests no network permission, and contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or social-login services, so there is no sharing of data with the developer or any third party.

Countmin has no cloud sync and no system-mediated data upload. All core features work offline; moving data between devices relies on the local backup you export and import yourself.

5. Children's privacy

Countmin is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the method below and we will delete it promptly.

6. Deleting and controlling your data

Your data is always under your control, and you can manage it at any time:

  • Delete individual records, notes, or cards within the app; deleted items go to a trash and can be emptied after a retention period.
  • Use "More → Data Management" to export a JSON backup, import a backup, or view / empty the trash.
  • Tap "Clear All Data" under "Settings → About" to reset local records. This cannot be undone.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes local data. With no cloud sync, the developer cannot restore it for you.

We have no ability to access, view, export, or recover this data on your device.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app's features change in a way that affects how data is handled, we will update this page and provide the latest privacy-policy link within the app. The updated policy applies from the effective date shown on this page.
